## 👋 About me

I'm a student at **HSE University** and a developer who builds **data analytics tools end to end**: from statistics and data cleaning to the interfaces people actually enjoy using. I like taking messy tables and turning them into clear answers: what drives what, where the data is broken, and what to do next.

- 🔭 Building **[Analitik Graph](https://github.com/Sena22555/analitik-graph)**, a workspace that maps data as causes and effects
- 📊 Into BI, statistics, data visualization and product analytics
- 🧰 Daily tools: Python, Java, SQL, pandas, Spring Boot, Qt
- 🎓 Studying at HSE University
- 💬 Happy to talk data: [@sensey_19](https://t.me/sensey_19)
